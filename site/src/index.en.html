Features

Everything a team needs, week after week.

Started in a handball club where several hundred players, parents and coaches use it every day. Kabine is the same app for any club and any sport.

Players

See trainings, games and special events, say yes or no with one tap, and get notified when something changes. Trainings count as accepted until someone declines: nobody has to answer every week.

Parents

Respond for their children, follow the team chat, take helper duties and offer seats for away games. They decide what their child may do in the app.

Coaches

Set the training times once a season. The app creates the trainings and skips holidays. Nominate squads, cancel, move, invite by link, see who cannot be reached yet.

Admins

Set up the club with a wizard: language, region, sport, teams, modules, e-mail. Manage people and do the season change once a year.

Helper duties

Cake, cash desk, referees: duties for home games with templates and a live plan; parents sign up themselves.

Carpools

Parents offer seats, players book one. The group gets the meeting point in time before the game.

Modules for the club

Fixtures from handball4all, games from spreadsheets, youth referees, sign-in codes by SMS. All switchable.

Privacy and youth protection

Most users are children.

Of players and parents Kabine stores only first name, last name and, for players, the birth year. No address, no date of birth, no phone number, unless someone enters one for SMS.

  • E-mail and phone number are entirely optional and only for signing in. Other users, coaches included, never see them. Admins see only other admins' e-mail addresses and, in the data export, the accounts' e-mail addresses; phone numbers never.
  • Decline reasons are visible to coaches and admins only and deleted 60 days after the event.
  • Parents decide what their child may do in the app and approve profile pictures.
  • Abbreviated names for helper duties and carpools (“Kim M.”).
  • Coaches are in every chat. There are no private messages.
  • No tracking, no ads, no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Cloudflare as processor in the club's own account. The database should be created explicitly in Europe (--jurisdiction eu); the chat already lives in the EU.

Deliberately an internal app

One club, one installation.

  • The data stays with the club. Each club runs its copy in its own Cloudflare account. No central server, and the developers have no access to any club’s data.
  • No app store. Open the address, add it to the home screen, done. With notifications, on the iPhone too. Every update reaches everyone at once.
  • Invitation only. No public sign-up. People sign in with a passkey (Face ID, fingerprint, screen lock), no passwords.
  • Lean. Membership fees, bookkeeping and the club website stay where they are.

Installation

About 30 minutes, in your own account.

You need a Cloudflare account (the free plan is enough for a typical club), a GitHub account and ideally a domain of the club. The button creates your own copy and all resources; you only choose a password for the first admin. Updates arrive as pull requests in your copy.
