2. Hosting on Cloudflare

Both websites run on Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, Cloudflare necessarily processes technical connection data: your IP address, date and time, the requested address, browser and operating system. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the websites.

Cloudflare acts as a processor under a data processing agreement. Data may be transferred to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR).

3. Cloudflare bot protection

On both websites Cloudflare sets the cookie __cf_bm to recognise automated access (bots). It holds an identifier that Cloudflare derives from properties of the connection and expires after 30 minutes. If a request looks suspicious, Cloudflare may show a check page instead of the page. A Cloudflare script runs in your browser there and evaluates technical properties of your browser and device; after a passed check Cloudflare sets the cookie cf_clearance, which by default expires after 30 minutes. Cloudflare does not insert a script into the normal pages.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is protecting the websites against misuse. Accessing your device for this is strictly necessary (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

4. kabine.dev

Apart from the cookie in section 3, the website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising and loads no fonts, scripts, images or other content from other servers. The only exception is Cloudflare Turnstile on the contact page (section 6).

If you switch the language or close the hint about the other language version, your browser stores this choice in localStorage (entry kabine-site-lang). It stays on your device until you delete it in your browser settings and is not sent to me. Storing it is strictly necessary for the language choice you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Links to other websites (such as GitHub) are plain links. Only when you click one does your browser connect to the other website; it then sends only the address kabine.dev, not the page you came from. From then on the other provider's privacy policy applies.

5. Contact form

If you use the contact form, I receive your name, email address and message. I use them only to answer your request. The form is delivered to my mailbox by email (Cloudflare Email Sending) and is not stored in a database; its content is not logged either. My email provider stores the message in my mailbox as a processor. I delete the message once your request has been dealt with, unless I am legally required to keep it.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is responding to your request.

6. Spam protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

The contact page uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the form against bots. For this the page loads a Cloudflare script (challenges.cloudflare.com) that evaluates technical information about your browser and device. Turnstile is loaded only on the contact page.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is protecting the form against abuse. Accessing this information on your device is strictly necessary to provide the contact form you are using (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Cloudflare processes the data as described in section 2.

7. Demo on handball.kabine.dev

The demo shows the app with an invented club. You use it without signing up, through shared demo access (player, parent, coach, admin) that all visitors use together. Whatever you enter there is seen by other visitors. Please do not enter real personal data. Email, SMS, passkeys, profile pictures, uploading a club logo, feedback and the game schedules from handball4all are switched off in the demo.

Every night at 3:00 (German time) the demo is reset: everything visitors entered is deleted on the server and the invented club is created again.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is presenting the software and letting people try it. Accessing your device (section 7.1) is strictly necessary for the demo to work the way you use it (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
