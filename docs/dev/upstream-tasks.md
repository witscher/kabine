# Tasks from the original club app

Kabine started as the app of one handball club, which is still developed on its own. New features and bug fixes made there are listed here so they can be brought into Kabine. Whoever works on Kabine takes an open entry, ports it the Kabine way (English source, Lingui, club profile instead of club data, modules, tests) and moves it to "Done" with the Kabine commit.

Entries describe the change, not the club: no names, domains or data of the club.

Format per entry: date, title, what changed and why, where in the code (file and function names are the same in both code bases unless noted), and anything that needs adapting for Kabine.

## Open

- **2026-11-14 Home: next event today gets a calm animation.** When the big "next event" card (also the coach variant) is today, its yellow header gets a soft light sweep every six seconds and "today" becomes a dark pill (like the team badges) with a gently pulsing dot and a soft glow, so it stands out without being loud; off with `prefers-reduced-motion` (`DayTag`, `.card-head.is-today` in `src/pages/home.tsx` and `src/styles.css`).

- **2026-11-13 Polls: choice, scheduling and orders (in staging, not yet live in the club app).** Coaches ask their team or group, admins the whole club (all teams, no groups). Three kinds: choice (single or multiple answers), scheduling (yes/maybe/no per date, like Doodle) and order (items with price, optional sizes, quantity). Who votes: players (group members), parents (teams with parents only), coaches, combinable; for players in teams with parents the parents also vote for their child, for orders only the parents. Optional "one vote per family": everyone connected through parent links (parents, children, siblings, patchwork) is one unit with a shared, editable ballot ("last changed by …"). Eligible units live in `poll_voters` (unit, member, may vote) and are re-synced hourly while a poll runs, keeping unit ids so ballots survive (`computeVoters`, `syncVoters`, `runPolls` in `worker/domain/polls.ts`; migration `0033_polls.sql`). Votes can be changed and withdrawn until the end. Results "visible now" or "after the end"; creator, team coaches and admins always see them. Names: managers see names and who is missing unless anonymous; participants see names only for non-anonymous scheduling polls, as "First L." (an exception to the names-only-to-coaches rule, documented as a club decision). Anonymous: nobody sees who voted what, the unit is still stored so ballots stay editable and reminders go only to missing voters; orders cannot be anonymous. Orders: the creator (admins as fallback) ticks "paid" per order; no free text. Payment: club-wide setting under admin → polls (admins only), stored in `app_meta` key `poll_payment`: optional PayPal.me name (only paypal.me names; the app opens paypal.me with the family's amount) and/or account holder with IBAN (checksum validated); applies to every order poll. Voters who ordered see amount, PayPal button, IBAN and reference with copy buttons and an EPC QR code (GiroCode, rendered in the app with `uqr`). Bank reference is the title plus an order number assigned on the first order and kept on changes (`poll_ballots.order_no`, migration `0034_poll_payment.sql`), so no names end up on bank statements; the number also shows in the creator's order list. paypal.me cannot prefill a message, so tapping the PayPal button copies a note with order number and the players' full names (children included) to the clipboard, also shown with a copy button (`paymentReference`, `PollUnit.paypalNote`, `PaymentBox`, `ClubPaymentSettings`). The Kabine-format export drops the `poll_payment` key until Kabine has it. Where it shows: a card at the top of the home screen while a vote is missing, counted in the app icon badge (`badgeCount`), a system message with a link in the team chat (new `link` column in the chat Durable Object's `messages`, only `/umfragen/<id>` paths), push at start and once 24 h before the end to missing voters (new notification topic `polls`, own account only; migration rebuilds `notification_settings` for the CHECK). Lists under profile, team/group page and admin. Season change deletes all polls; not in the export yet. Tests: `test/polls.test.ts`, e2e "Umfrage", "Bestellung", "Auswahl". In Kabine: fits the planned "polls" module on the roadmap (switchable), texts via Lingui, privacy text for stored answers, and the export.

- **2026-11-12 Helpers from other teams ("help out") and a switch for "helpers wanted".** At adult games, youth teams help out (e.g. floor wipers from under-11 and under-13 teams), other adults as stewards. "Who helps" gets "Teams" with a second step to pick single teams (`duty_type_teams`, migration `0032_duty_teams.sql`), combinable with parents/players/coaches/members. The player of a picked team does the duty at any event where it appears; for children with involved parents a parent signs the child up ("Who helps?"), the entry then names the child (as for youth referees), children with their own account sign up themselves (`teamRolesFor`, `mergeRoles` in `worker/domain/duties.ts`; `HelperRole.doer`). The helper tab gets a list "Help out" (like the referee list) with upcoming events of other teams and only these duties; template blocks of the next 60 days are generated for it (`GET /duties/help-out`, `Me.helpOut` shows the tab even without an own helper plan). A person with such a duty (or one the coach assigned) sees that event in the events list without participant list; an ordinary duty still does not make a foreign event visible (`relatesSql`). With "assigned by the coach" the picker includes players of the picked teams; an assigned child is linked as child so parents see, get notified and can remove it. New switch on every duty type "Send ‘helpers wanted’" (`duty_types.notify_open`, on for existing types): decided on the duty type, not the template, because one duty is in several templates and manual blocks have none; it covers all possible helpers incl. other teams (`teamHelperAccounts` in `worker/domain/duty-notify.ts`). When only one duty is wanted it is in the title ("Wiper wanted · Men 1"). 3- and 1-day reminders go to the parents when the child does the duty. New switch "Adults only" on the duty type (`duty_types.adults_only`, same migration, off for existing types), e.g. for stewards: whoever is under 18 by birth year (current year minus birth year) cannot sign up, is not offered or accepted for assignment (error `adults_only`) and gets no "helpers wanted"; this applies to every "who helps" path, not only teams. No birth year counts as adult (mostly parents and coaches). With "parents" the parent counts, not the child; when a parent signs up their child via "Teams", the child counts (`adultRoles`, `adultSql`, `rolesForActor` in `worker/domain/duties.ts`; the actor carries its own and its children's birth years). No new personal data, only the existing birth year. Tests in `test/duties.test.ts` ("Helfer aus anderen Mannschaften", incl. "Nur Erwachsene"), `test/duty-notify.test.ts`, `test/visibility.test.ts`. In Kabine: duties module, texts via Lingui, the export omits the new table/columns until then.

- **2026-11-12 Duty types the coach assigns.** Some duties nobody should pick themselves; the coach assigns them (e.g. for adult teams). New switch on the duty type, "Assigned by the coach" (`duty_types.assign_only`, migration `0031_duty_assign_only.sql`), independent of "needs prior knowledge" (where an admin approves people who then sign up themselves). Nobody signs up themselves (`rolesFor` returns no role unless the person manages the team; `POST /duties` answers 403 `assign_only`); the helper tab shows "assigned by the coach" on free slots. Coaches, admins and organisers assign through "enter someone", and "who helps" still applies: the picker (`GET /duties/events/:id/people?type=<id>`) only lists matching people and the server rejects others with 400 `not_in_who` (`whoSql(type, person, teamSet)` in `worker/domain/duties.ts`, same rules as `rolesFor`; for all other duty types "enter someone" stays open to anyone in the club). A name without account stays possible. The assigned person gets a notification right away, "You are assigned: <duty>" with time and who assigned, over the channel of the 3-day reminder (`sendDutyAssigned` in `worker/domain/duty-notify.ts`), and may remove themselves. No "helpers wanted" for these duties, they do not count as "helpers needed"; coaches still get the open-slot reminder. Tests in `test/duties.test.ts` ("Dienst, den der Trainer vergibt") and `test/duty-notify.test.ts`, e2e. In Kabine this belongs to the duties module; texts through Lingui.

- **2026-11-12 Duty block duration 2.5 h.** The duration chips of a new time block get 150 minutes ("2,5 h") between 2 h and 3 h (`DURATIONS` in `src/duty-blocks.tsx`).
